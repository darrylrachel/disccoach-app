# DiscCoach Product Vision

## Product Name

DiscCoach

## Vision Statement

DiscCoach is the personal improvement platform for disc golfers.

The mission is to help players become better golfers by combining intelligent disc management, structured practice, training programs, and measurable performance tracking into one complete experience.

Disc golf improvement should not be based on guessing, buying random discs, or practicing without direction.

DiscCoach gives players the knowledge, structure, and feedback they need to improve intentionally.

---

# The Problem

Most disc golfers struggle with improvement because they lack three things:

## 1. Disc Knowledge

Players often own many discs but do not fully understand:

-   What each disc does
-   Why they should throw it
-   How discs change over time
-   Which discs overlap
-   What discs they are missing

Many players buy new discs hoping they will solve problems that are actually caused by technique or poor bag construction.

## 2. Structured Practice

Most players know they should practice but do not know:

-   What drills to do
-   How often to practice
-   How to measure improvement
-   What weaknesses to focus on

Practice often becomes random field work or casual putting without a clear goal.

## 3. Measurable Progress

Players lack a complete picture of improvement.

They want to know:

-   Am I throwing farther?
-   Is my putting improving?
-   Which discs perform best for me?
-   What areas should I focus on next?

Disc golfers need a system that turns practice into progress.

---

# Core Product Pillars

# 1. Smart Disc Management

The foundation of DiscCoach.

Players create a digital representation of their disc collection.

The app stores:

-   Manufacturer
-   Mold
-   Plastic
-   Weight
-   Flight numbers
-   Condition
-   Personal notes
-   Personal flight characteristics

The key difference:

DiscCoach understands that every disc becomes unique based on:

-   Plastic type
-   Wear
-   Player arm speed
-   Throwing style
-   Technique

The player's version of a disc matters more than manufacturer numbers.

---

# 2. Intelligent Bag Building

DiscCoach helps players build smarter bags.

The app analyzes:

-   Disc overlap
-   Flight coverage
-   Stability gaps
-   Missing shot shapes
-   Speed gaps

Instead of asking:

"What disc should I buy?"

Players understand:

"What does my current bag need?"

The goal is not owning more discs.

The goal is having the right discs.

---

# 3. Purposeful Practice

Practice Mode is the heart of DiscCoach.

Players should never open the app and wonder:

"What should I practice today?"

DiscCoach generates structured sessions based on:

-   Available time
-   Player goals
-   Skill level
-   Previous performance

Examples:

20-minute putting session

45-minute distance session

Field work session

Accuracy training

Practice becomes intentional, measurable, and repeatable.
